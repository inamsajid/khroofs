Roof Replacement

Our well-adapted team have a wealth of experience replacing both slate and tiled roofs across Manchester. We source top-grade materials, delivering 30 to 50 years of hassle-free durability.

  • High-performance breathable roofing felt/membrane
  • Treated timber battens & dry verge capping
  • Competitively priced to industry rates
GRP & EPDM

Flat Roof Installation

Flat roofing requires a special set of skills. We classically replace flat roofs with high-quality materials including GRP fibreglass or EPDM rubber with up to 50 years expected maintenance-free life.

  • Seamless GRP fibreglass waterproofing
  • Heavy-duty Firestone EPDM rubber systems
  • Ideal for extensions, garages & dormers
Heritage & Modern

Slating & Tiling

No two roofs are the same. Our expert specialists evaluate your property to advise which action and slate/tile material best fits your specific roof architecture and budget.

  • Welsh, Spanish & reclaimed natural slates
  • Interlocking concrete & clay roof tiles
  • Pledge of optimal craftsmanship quality
UPVC Solutions

Fascia & Guttering

We fit and supply all types of UPVC fascia boards, soffits, dry verges, guttering, and cladding in square or round profiles. We also clean and maintain systems to remove winter debris.

  • Zero-maintenance UPVC fascias & soffits
  • Seamless gutter replacements & realignments
  • Springtime clearing & fixed component repairs
